## 3. Tricky words by phase

- Phase 2 Autumn 1 [C3][C1]: is, I, the.
- Phase 2 Autumn 2 [C1]: as, and, has, his, her, go, no, to, into, she, he, of, we, me, be; put, pull, full, push (asterisked: may be treated as decodable in some accents).
- Phase 3 Spring 1 [C1]: was, you, they, my, by, all, are, sure, pure.
- Phase 4 Summer 1 [C1]: said, so, have, like, some, come, love, do, were, here, little, says, there, when, what, one, out, today. Weekly split in summaries: wk1 said so have like; wk2 some come love do; wk3 were here little says; wk4 there when what one; wk5 out today. Versions differ (older ones omit love, here, says, today).
- Year 1 Autumn 2 [C5]: their, people, oh, your, Mr, Mrs, Ms, ask, could, would, should, our, house, mouse, water, want. "ask" may not be tricky in some accents.
- Further Year 1 tricky words (Spring/Summer): not obtained. UNCERTAIN.

## 4. Terminology and teaching principles

Fetched-via-summary: GPC, grapheme, phoneme, digraph, trigraph, split digraph ("a vowel digraph that is split by a consonant", e.g. a-e, i-e, o-e, u-e, e-e) [C2]; tricky words; Phase 2-5 labelling; review of earlier phases at the start of Year 1 [C2].
INFERRED (general Letters and Sounds / Little Wandle knowledge, not verified from a fetched page): pure sounds (no added "uh"); oral blending then sound-blending (sound-talk, "Fred talk"-style terms are used by Little Wandle but were NOT verified here, so avoid relying on them); Speed Sounds is a different programme (Read Write Inc), not Little Wandle; keep-up/review cycles; decodable books matched to taught GPCs; short daily lessons with revisit, practise, apply structure; assessment every six weeks.
